# Launch Lab

## Prompt

"Make a projectile motion lab where students predict the launch angle to hit a target, auto-graded, with the target distance set by the teacher."

## Follow-up answers

What the teacher said when /zest-new asked its follow-up questions, in their words:

- **Subject and grade:** "Physics, grades 9 to 12."
- **Grading:** "Auto-graded. Each round, their predicted angle either hits the target or it
  doesn't. The grade is how many targets they hit out of how many rounds."
- **Save progress:** "Yes, save their progress so they can come back to it."
- **Settings I want to change per class (parameters):**
  - "The target distances, one per round. Default something like 18, 27, 36, 24 and 33 meters."
  - "The launch speed. 20 m/s is fine to start."
  - "How close counts as a hit. 1.5 meters either side."
  - "A switch to turn on an aiming guide, a dotted line and a hint that two different
    angles can hit the same target. Off by default."
  - "Make sure every target can actually be reached at that speed."
- **How a round works:** "They have to commit to a predicted angle first, then launch. Show
  the ball flying on an arc with the launcher turning to the angle, where it lands, the
  distance, and whether it was a hit or how far short or long. Only the predicted launch
  counts. After that let them take practice shots that don't count, so they can explore
  before moving on. That's where they'll find out that two angles can land in the same spot."
- **What I see in SpeedGrader:** "Every round's scored shot drawn against its target on one
  field, hits and misses in different colors, a table of the rounds, and their practice
  shots greyed out."
- **Refinements after the first look:** "Make it look great on a phone too." "Put the range
  formula on the screen, small, for students who want it." "Show a short summary of all the
  rounds at the end."
