# Label the Plant Cell (sketch-label)

## Prompt

"Students label a plant cell by drawing arrows and writing the names of the parts right on the diagram, then explain in a sentence what the chloroplast does. I'll grade it myself, and in SpeedGrader I want to see their drawing and watch how they drew it."

## Follow-up answers

Plain-language answers to /zest-new's questions and the refinements asked for, in the teacher's words:

- **Subject and level:** Life science, middle school and high school.
- **Grading:** "I'll grade it myself." (Teacher-graded; the work goes to SpeedGrader.)
- **Save progress:** "Yes. Save their drawing and labels as they go, so nothing is lost if they close the tab."
- **The diagram:** "A clean, accurate plant cell with no labels on it: cell wall, cell membrane, nucleus with the nucleolus, the big central vacuole, chloroplasts, mitochondria and cytoplasm. ER and ribosomes are fine too. Make it look nice, soft greens. It has to be scientifically correct."
- **Tools:** "A pen with a few colours and a thin and thick line, an arrow they can drag, and a label tool where they tap, type the name and can drag it around. Eraser, undo and redo, and a Clear all that asks twice. It has to work with a finger on a phone or tablet and with a mouse."
- **Checklist:** "Show the list of parts they need to label next to the diagram."
- **Settings I can change:** "Let me change the list of parts (default: cell wall, cell membrane, nucleus, chloroplast, vacuole, mitochondrion) and the question (default: What does the chloroplast do?)."
- **Submitting:** "Don't let them submit until they have at least one label and an answer, and make them tap twice to submit."
- **What I see when grading:** "Their finished drawing, full size, and a replay I can play, pause, speed up (1x, 2x, 4x) and scrub through, stroke by stroke, with the time shown. Next to it their answer, which parts they labelled, and how long they spent. A small picture of the finished drawing too."
- **Refinement:** "Make it work nicely on a phone; the toolbar has to fit."
